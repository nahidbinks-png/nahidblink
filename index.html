Group History

BLACKPINK (Korean: 블랙핑크) is a South Korean girl group formed by YG Entertainment, consisting of members Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. The group debuted in August 2016 with their single album Square One, which featured "Whistle" and "Boombayah", their first number-one entries on South Korea's Gaon Digital Chart and the US Billboard World Digital Song Sales chart, respectively.

They have broken numerous online records throughout their career. Their music video for "Ddu-Du Ddu-Du" was the most-viewed music video by a Korean group for a long time, and they became the first Korean group to headline Coachella. They continue to dominate the global music industry as fashion icons and musical powerhouses.

The Fandom: BLINK

The official fandom name for BLACKPINK is BLINK, a portmanteau of the words "Black" and "Pink". The name signifies how the group started with Black and ended with Pink, representing their connection with their fans.

Official Color: Black & Pink

World Tour History

  • Blackpink Arena Tour (2018) - Their debut concert tour in Japan.
  • In Your Area World Tour (2018–2020) - Their first major global tour spanning Asia, North America, Europe, and Oceania.
  • Born Pink World Tour (2022–2023) - A massive stadium-filling world tour that broke multiple attendance and revenue records for a female group.
